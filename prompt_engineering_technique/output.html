# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to support marathon training with high-calorie endurance nutrition:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (115g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), sunflower seeds (25g), and whole milk (240ml). Honey (20g).
  * Protein: 15g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Whole wheat bread (60g) with peanut butter (30g) and jam (20g). Orange juice (250ml).
  * Protein: 12g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 65g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil and vegetable curry with chickpeas (150g cooked), spinach (100g), carrot (80g), onion (60g), and coconut milk (100ml). Basmati rice (150g cooked). Side of plain yogurt (100g).
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 85g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Energy bar made with oats (1 bar, 60g), dried dates (40g), and seeds (15g). Smoothie with banana (120g), yogurt (150g), and honey (15g).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 95g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked tofu (180g) with roasted sweet potato (250g), broccoli (150g), and olive oil (15ml). Whole grain pasta (100g cooked) with marinara sauce (100g).
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 70g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Whole wheat crackers (50g) with hummus (50g) and cucumber slices (100g). Chamomile tea with milk (240ml).
  * Protein: 8g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 20g
